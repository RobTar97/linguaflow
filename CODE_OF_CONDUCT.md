# Code of Conduct

LinguaFlow is a learning community. We expect participation to be welcoming,
patient, curious, and respectful across differences in language ability,
culture, identity, experience, and technical background.

## Expected behavior

- Give specific, constructive feedback.
- Assume language mistakes are part of learning.
- Respect names, pronouns, boundaries, and cultural context.
- Make room for new contributors and less confident speakers.
- Protect learner and classroom privacy.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
humiliation, doxxing, repeated disruption, and publishing private information
without permission are not tolerated.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the
host platform’s private contact or security-reporting channel. Maintainers may
remove content, restrict participation, or ban contributors when necessary.
Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

This policy applies in project repositories, discussions, events, and other
spaces where someone is representing LinguaFlow.
