# Roadmap

This roadmap describes direction, not delivery dates.

## Foundation — version 0.1

- role and language setup;
- multilingual topic library;
- learner browsing and guided sessions;
- teacher room builder;
- cross-device Cloudflare room synchronization;
- content validation, documentation, and accessibility baseline.

## Classroom hardening

- authenticated teacher and learner profiles;
- participant reconnect and explicit leave behavior;
- teacher locks, moderation, and session expiry;
- privacy and data-retention controls.

## Curriculum platform

- reviewable external topic packs;
- richer CEFR and skill metadata;
- content contribution tooling;
- translation-review workflow;
- educator notes and lesson timing;
- import/export for community-maintained curricula.

## Learning continuity

- session history controlled by the learner;
- vocabulary review and personal notes;
- goals and progress without high-pressure streak mechanics;
- accessible audio support;
- optional self-reflection after conversations.

## Explicitly later

Automated speech scoring, AI conversation partners, institutional dashboards,
and marketplace features require separate evidence, safety work, and product
decisions. They are not assumptions embedded in the current architecture.
